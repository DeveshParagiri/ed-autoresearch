# LAI packing and phase-depletion overlap audit at `eb4dddf`

## Verdict

LAI/canopy bulk-density gating is already tested and should not be reopened with the current inputs. A phase-anchored depletion gate is not an exact duplicate, but nearly all of its state and feedback already exist; the only untested information is a strictly causal rising-versus-falling phase selector. That selector is suitable for one bounded diagnostic, not yet for a canonical physical claim.

## LAI/canopy packing overlap

The incumbent already uses tall-canopy by high-LAI interaction as a humid closed-fuel brake at `autoresearch/model.py:352-387`. It repeatedly uses canopy height to partition open and woody fuel, including local footprint at `1857-1938`, pathway shares and moisture at `2304-2469`, fuel recovery at `2473-2574`, secondary litter at `2578-2727`, and final live/dead timing at `1483-1611`. The final timing law explicitly combines `leaf_support=LAI/(LAI+2)` with `open_structure=8/(canopy+8)` and woody structure at `1532-1542`.

More decisively, the scratch Rothermel closure already formed `packing=LAI/(LAI+2)`, `depth=bed_height/(bed_height+8)`, and `bulk_density=(0.10+packing)/(0.25+depth)` at `autoresearch/scratchpad/rothermel_event_closure_121c83c.py:137-156`. Rate of spread used this bulk-density proxy in its heat sink, and all 27 fixed moisture, footprint, and weak-blend brackets failed the four held annual/allocation/raw-cycle gates. The result is recorded in `autoresearch/scratchpad/thread.md:6180-6194`. A second, genuinely different surface-area construction kept LAI in native one-sided-area units and also failed coupled timing gates, as recorded at `thread.md:6414-6428`.

The physics does not justify inventing another LAI/canopy mapping. Rothermel defines packing ratio as fuel-bed bulk density divided by fuel-particle density and weights mixed fuel inputs by particle surface area. The later Forest Service technical synthesis states that bulk density is load divided by fuel-bed depth, that mean packing ratio spans roughly 0.00106 to 0.04878 across its 53 standard fuel models, and that optimum packing depends on particle surface-area-to-volume ratio. These definitions are documented in the original [US Forest Service Rothermel report](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/32533) and the [Forest Service comprehensive technical explanation](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/55928.pdf). Current `INPUTS` at `model.py:18-24` have grid-cell LAI, canopy height, and aggregate biomass, but not fine-fuel bed depth, particle density, size-class surface-area-to-volume ratio, or live/dead class load. LAI and canopy therefore cannot identify a physical packing ratio; another smooth gate would be coefficient substitution, not new mechanism state.

## Phase-anchored depletion overlap

Generic depletion has been tested repeatedly. The earliest global stock collapsed spatial skill and was tuned off, recorded at `thread.md:101-169` and preserved in the current warning at `model.py:344-349`. A corrected per-cell post-transform retest was monotonically harmful at every strength (`thread.md:2004-2045`). A within-season mean-neutral consumption law then accumulated recent burning and suppressed the falling limb exactly as proposed, but seasonal phase fell from 0.8679 to 0.7877 because the peak moved earlier (`thread.md:2884-2941`). Later prognostic connectivity and burnable-fraction stocks recovered from local fuel capacity and were depleted after candidate fire; strict footprint, centered-anomaly, annual-stock, and recoverable-opportunity roles all failed joint gates (`thread.md:5501-5537` and `5573-5618`).

The incumbent now contains three more direct overlaps. `_dead_fuel_pool_response` consumes fine and woody stores with local burn pressure at `model.py:1333-1480`. `_pathway_fuel_recovery_reservoir` recovers surface, crop, and woody stocks on globally shared 6-, 24-, and 120-month clocks, then depletes them after their current burn at `2473-2574`. `_live_dead_litter_timing`, now the final stage in the call chain at `2875-2880`, consumes dead fast and slow stores after each current event at `1483-1611`. The surface and multipath opportunity banks at `1747-1854` and `2189-2469` already conserve and defer finite hazard by local readiness.

What has not been tested is conditioning the effect of prior consumption on a causal phase turning point. The earlier consumption laws allow burn history to act whenever stock is low; none uses the sign of a causal combustibility tendency to distinguish the rising limb from the falling limb. A completed-year peak month, calendar month, latitude, or future argmax would be invalid. The only admissible anchor is a current local readiness crossing computed from the existing prefix, such as the surface-bank physical window at `1830-1844`.

This narrow novelty is weaker than it first appears. Fire physically consumes fuel whenever it burns, not only after a seasonal maximum. Field experiments in Kruger show that annually burned savanna plots retained substantial fuel and that seasonal moisture could dominate the intensity response to fuel-load differences; the primary study is [Govender et al. 2006](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2664.2006.01184.x). Modern SPITFIRE analysis also identifies grid-cell-average fuel removal as a structural problem: scaling whole-cell fuel consumption by burned fraction merely skims the average pool, whereas patch-resolved stands preserve strong local post-fire feedback. That limitation is described in the primary [LPJmL-SPITFIRE1.9 analysis](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/18/2021/2025/). A phase gate cannot recover the absent burned-patch state.

## Highest-information formulation

If one further scratch test is warranted, it should isolate only the phase selector and include an otherwise identical unanchored depletion control. Reuse the incumbent surface pathway share and define the causal readiness `C_t` from the already installed fine-fuel, rain-deficit, combustion, and curing window rather than adding another LAI/canopy gate. Define bounded rising and falling tendencies as `R_t=max(C_t-C_(t-1),0)/(C_t+C_(t-1)+eps)` and `F_t=max(C_(t-1)-C_t,0)/(C_t+C_(t-1)+eps)`. Accumulate a prior-burn debt only from the surface share, `D_t=exp(-1/tau)D_(t-1)+S_(t-1)H_(t-1)`, and test `H'_t=H_t*exp(-a*S_t*F_t*D_t/(D_t+h))` against the control without `F_t`. Use only fixed weak `a` brackets and one existing recovery time, then require prefix exactness, the four held annual/allocation/raw-cycle losses, exact Overall components, region breadth, and intact tropical, boreal, tropical-open, arid, and Congo audits.

This equation is pointwise and globally shared: the same `a`, `tau`, and `h` apply everywhere, while `S`, `C`, and `H` are current local states. It contains no region, coordinate, calendar, target, or fitted phase. Its information value is binary. If the anchored law clearly outperforms the no-anchor control without moving peak phase, the phase selector adds something not present in prior depletion. If both behave alike or the anchored version repeats the seasonal loss, the family is closed. Even a positive diagnostic should remain a proxy until native patch/cohort fuel state is available, because the phase gate is not a substitute for fuel-bed load and burned-patch provenance.

The recommendation is therefore to reject another LAI/canopy packing experiment and, at most, run this one controlled phase-selector falsification. Do not tune packing constants or promote phase-depletion physics from current aggregate inputs alone.
