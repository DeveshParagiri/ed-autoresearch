# Zoom-out pathway headroom and LUH2 disturbance-age falsification

The audit is pinned to pruned canonical model blob `39ee93ebf1155af9ae9d70e05847b9c3f086887d`. A 1,536-cell chunked pass assembled a 49,766,528-byte scratch prediction over 18,316 land cells. Selective re-prediction is bit-identical, and the exact evaluator reproduces Overall 0.718363408, bias 0.756508969, RMSE 0.547448928, seasonal 0.861681586, and spatial 0.878728630. The pass took 42.85 seconds and peaked at 2.103 GiB RSS.

The held-block audit uses all 2,235 cells carrying 85.0033601% of observed-fire weight. Coordinates assign four 15-degree spatial folds but are not features. All eighteen features are ecological pathway interactions built from current or causal-memory inputs. The completed-year precipitation field, regions, coordinates, calendar, target-derived runtime state, neighbours, and modern invalid forcings are excluded. LUH2 secondary is also excluded because its installed aggregate is not a valid compositional share.

The empirical exact held-out headroom is small. Annual-map correction alone reaches 0.721889859, a gain of 0.003526451. Its components are bias 0.763001210, RMSE 0.548354074, seasonal 0.861633548, and spatial 0.888106390. Normalized-cycle correction alone reaches 0.719521645, a gain of 0.001158237, with bias 0.756542021, RMSE 0.549639902, seasonal 0.862991235, and spatial 0.878795164. Their best joint correction reaches 0.723182076, a gain of 0.004818667, with bias 0.763006838, RMSE 0.550892790, seasonal 0.862991235, and spatial 0.888126726. These learned corrections are diagnostic only and are not model candidates.

Annual-map MAE improves in every held block. At depth three it falls from 0.810299 to 0.514151, 0.890603 to 0.570281, 0.686326 to 0.514059, and 0.676747 to 0.503001. Cycle MAE is not stable: it improves in folds one and three but worsens in folds zero and two. Most remaining honest headroom is therefore annual spatial allocation, not a globally shared seasonal clock.

Stable annual pathways are humid woody shielding, managed-open opportunity, warm-open recurrence, recurrent surface opportunity, cold-open thaw, and urban-transition fragmentation. Stable pairs include managed-open opportunity with warm recurrence, cold thaw with warm recurrence, recurrent surface opportunity with urban expansion, and LUH2 turnover with warm recurrence. Cycle trees rank warming-drying ignition, live-to-dead transition, cold thaw, and rain-built drydown, but their aggregate held performance reverses across folds.

A one-dimensional sign audit shows that `turnover x warm recurrence` reduces annual-map MAE in every block by 0.226307, 0.230548, 0.131653, and 0.113006. A smooth stable-managed-recurrence composite is nearly as predictive, improving all blocks by 0.225631, 0.236562, 0.124939, and 0.089939. However, annual underprediction decreases monotonically as that composite rises, from +2.532 in the lowest positive quintile to +0.098 in the highest. Conversion-residue, crop expansion, grazing expansion, and abandonment terms reverse across folds, and transition-only cycle learners are flat or negative.

The only distinct mechanism suggested by that ranking was a LUH2 disturbance-age fuel-continuity state. Absolute causal changes in primary, crop, pasture, rangeland, and urban cover enter a finite 24-month memory. A warm, rain-supported, managed-open Poisson-hazard capacity boost then recovers smoothly as that disturbance memory decays. The equation has no recurrence term; recurrence is used only for post-prediction strata.

The bounded sampled test rejects that interpretation. At strength 0.5, disturbance-age capacity improves high-weight annual log error from 0.842724 to 0.833033 in all four blocks and raises the modeled-to-observed ratio from 0.8561 to 0.8937. Yet the identical managed-open capacity without turnover memory reaches 0.827434 and improves every fold more strongly. The transition state removes roughly 37% of the useful signal. Its annual gain appears in every recurrence quartile, confirming that broad managed-open capacity rather than disturbance age drives the result.

The disturbance-age equation also worsens aggregate seasonal L1 by 0.000417. Its ecological effects at strength 0.5 are intact tropical closed 0.887 to 0.912, temperate closed 1.017 to 1.064, boreal 1.108 to 1.108, tropical open 0.981 to 1.032, productive rangeland 0.928 to 0.961, cropland 0.912 to 0.919, and arid low fuel 1.199 to 1.201. The prefix perturbation is bit-exact. Because the no-memory control is superior and duplicates the previously failed managed-capacity and partitioned-closure family, neither form clears the threshold for an exact full-grid run.
