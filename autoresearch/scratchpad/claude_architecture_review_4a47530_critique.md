## What survives the critique

The strongest external diagnosis is that the current predictor is a product-rate followed by a long cascade of hazard rescalers, banks, capacity terms, and causal renormalizers that repeatedly recompute nearly the same fuel, openness, moisture, and recurrence states. The journal already measured large non-additivity, most notably the -.03980 interaction between pathway event scaling and seasonal rain capacity in Entry 123 and the -.002336 recurrence-by-annual-closure collision in Entry 146. The new stage-order win at Entry 177 is independent supporting evidence: the identical brake equation performs better merely by moving across one event-scaling operator. A large step is therefore more likely to come from replacing a topology or consolidating physical pathways than from adding a twentieth bounded multiplier.

The score target also survives. Current RMSE is .547470 and has double weight, while bias is .756875, seasonality .861688, and spatial skill .881606. Entry 122 showed that an annual-map oracle and a cycle oracle are individually insufficient for .8 but a half correction of both reaches .8102. Any credible structural family must therefore create independent annual-propensity and monthly-allocation degrees of freedom or improve both through a shared physical state. A family that only tweaks phase, only changes a low-fire tail, or only rescales annual mass is not high leverage.

## Strongest genuinely distinct families

### Prognostic connectivity controlling occurrence and event size

The strongest surviving idea is to replace the single rate with `BA = 1 - exp(-lambda * Abar)`, where ignition occurrence `lambda` and mean event footprint `Abar` are separate physical quantities. The genuinely new part is not occurrence-times-spread itself, which Entries 122, 123, and 162 already tested. It is a pointwise prognostic burnable-patch connectivity state `rho` that recovers toward a cover- and GPP-supported capacity, is depleted by realized burning, and controls event size through a bounded percolation response. Existing fuel reservoirs track availability or postponed hazard; they do not make a conserved subgrid connectivity state the sole scale of event footprint while keeping ignition occurrence separate.

This could jointly affect the double-weighted RMSE and the annual spatial map because frequent small fires and rare large events no longer share one scalar amplitude. The cheapest falsifier should remain scratch-only: derive a crude ignition opportunity from lightning plus managed access, integrate `rho` from inputs without using target data in the state, and ask in four held spatial blocks whether observed `BA / ignition_opportunity` increases monotonically with `rho`. A stable sign in at least three folds is the minimum evidence. The main failure is an artificial near-threshold explosion that behaves like a hidden geographic classifier. Event size must be globally capped, and tropical-open, productive-rangeland, boreal, arid-low-fuel, and country outliers must be audited before any canonical release.

### Fully resolved additive pathway hazards as a replacement topology

A second conditional survivor is a wholesale source topology `BA = 1 - exp[-(h_surface + h_woody + h_crop + h_background)]`, with each fuel class receiving its own fuel stock or capacity, ignition access, moisture readiness, and exposure exactly once. The novelty is narrower than Claude claimed. The current `_pathway_event_scaling` already forms a 35-percent resolved pathway mixture, the clean land-use rebuild already summed several independent hazards, and the finite-Poisson audit tested two unit-consistent unions. What has not been cleanly tested is a full current-input replacement in which the legacy product branch and the downstream stages subsumed by class-specific hazards are disabled together, while the current ED vegetation states are retained. The test is a topology replacement, not a larger value of `pathway_mix_w` and not another additive correction on top of the incumbent.

This family could relieve multiplicative vetoes that underburn score-dominant cells while allowing closed-canopy, crop, and woody guards to act only on the appropriate pathway. It should target bias and RMSE first and spatial skill second. The cheapest falsifier is a four-fold held-cell diagnostic with only four pathway scales fit on training blocks. It must beat the incumbent annual-log and cycle errors out of fold before any hand equation is installed. A fully additive source can reopen Congo and other humid closed-canopy fire unless humid canopy limits surface continuity as well as woody moisture. It must also avoid restoring the severe arid and boreal failures of prior direct rebuilds. This family is high-risk but still more informative than another late-stage modifier.

### Directional ignition-combustibility arrival order

The only genuinely new low-cost state from the corrective Claude pass is a signed trailing cross-lag between ignition and combustibility. A causal statistic can compare `EMA12(I_t C_{t-1})` with `EMA12(I_{t-1} C_t)` and use the normalized difference only to redistribute the existing surface and woody pathway shares. This is distinct from the contemporaneous combustion-temperature coherence term, the fixed lag on the assembled rate, and the same-month rare-onset equation. It asks whether ignition tends to arrive before or after the burnable window rather than shifting the prediction in time.

Its plausible target is spatial and centered-cycle RMSE at nearly neutral bias, but even Claude estimates only roughly +.0003. It is not a route to .8 by itself. The cheap falsifier is the sign-stability test on the four warm-open held blocks proposed in the revised response, followed by an explicit interaction check with annual closure. It should be abandoned immediately if fewer than three folds agree or if it redraws the annual map. This is suitable as a quick diagnostic while a larger replacement architecture is developed, not as the main research line.

## Rejected suggestions

The generic two-pool fuel budget is not novel. `unified_live_dead_fuel_02dc5d5.py` explicitly removed four bank/recovery stages and inserted a live/dead production, decay, moisture, and consumption state; `finite_fuel_event_rebuild_02dc5d5.py` was a direct standalone event model. Entries 122 and 134 tested produced-fuel and self-organizing prognostic variants. Claude's claim that all such tests left the whole downstream compensation stack active is factually false. A new fuel model requires a genuinely new observable state, not a renamed pool.

The proposed value-at-risk suppression feedback is also a duplicate. `_fragmented_managed_recurrence_brake` already multiplies crop/urban fragmentation, managed cover, and trailing realized hazard in a bounded negative feedback, while Entries 145-146 tested the broader managed recurrence equilibrium and found a destructive annual-closure interaction and spatial loss.

The lognormal dry-window exceedance proposal is not physically admissible as stated. Trailing twelve-month variance describes inter-month seasonal variability, not a sub-monthly distribution of fire-weather days. The same second moment is already active in coherent surface capacity. Calling it daily occupancy would invent temporal resolution absent from the input.

The actual-evapotranspiration two-channel proposal is attractive physics but cannot be computed as written from the present fields. The `dryness` input is a running Thornthwaite moisture deficit in millimetres, not a PET flux, so using it as `PET(t)` and subtracting it again in a new bucket double-counts an existing water-balance state. Earlier clean rebuilds and moisture probes already tested rain recharge plus dryness-driven drawdown. Without a separately valid PET or root-zone water flux, the claimed actual evapotranspiration is not identified.

The local asymmetric moisture-timescale idea is also closed for now. Rain-dependent lag failed monotonically in Entry 31, global hysteresis and consumption failed in Entries 92-94, Entry 145 showed that the apparent aggregate phase error is mainly an annual-map mixture, and the unified live/dead probe already blended cover-specific recovery times. There is no remaining falsifiable prediction that clearly separates the suggested form from those negatives.

## Recommendation

The best next architecture falsifier is the prognostic connectivity occurrence-size split because it creates the missing second degree of freedom and has a cheap held-block sign test. If that fails, the next serious experiment should be the fully resolved additive pathway replacement with every subsumed legacy stage disabled during the comparison. The arrival-order statistic is a small parallel diagnostic only. None justifies Optuna before a held-cell and exact mechanistic step, and none should receive official evaluation until the canonical path is globally shared, pointwise, prefix-exact, free of learned runtime surfaces, and ecologically bounded.
