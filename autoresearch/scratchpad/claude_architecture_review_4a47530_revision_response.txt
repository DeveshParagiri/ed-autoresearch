### E — Ignition-limited vs. fuel-limited *arrival order* within the month

**State/factorization.** Not a new pool and not a gate. The model computes ignition opportunity and combustion state at the same monthly instant and multiplies them. The new object is a causal scalar `φ ∈ [0,1]` measuring the *lead-lag alignment* between the ignition series and the combustibility series over trailing 12 months, built from cross-products already in the code: `EMA12(ignition·combustion_{t-1})` vs `EMA12(ignition_{t-1}·combustion)`, normalized by `EMA12(ignition)·EMA12(combustion)`. Where ignition *leads* combustibility, monthly burned area is ignition-limited and the product overstates it; where combustibility leads, fuel arrives pre-ignited and the product understates. `φ` re-weights the existing surface/woody split in `_pathway_event_scaling`, changing no capacity magnitude.

**Why prior experiments do not cover it.** `combustion_temperature_alignment` (`model.py:615-618`) is a *contemporaneous* alignment of combustion with temperature, not a signed lead-lag between ignition and combustibility. Entry 122's occurrence×spread factorization is same-month. Entry 31 rejected a fixed uniform lag on the *rate*; this changes no timing, only pathway share. Entry 94's phase coupling does not bind because argmax is untouched.

**Cheapest held-cell falsifier.** Compute `φ` on the four held blocks of Entry 145's 1,207 warm seasonal-open cells; require the sign of `φ`-residual correlation to be consistent in all four folds *before* any full-grid run. If fewer than four folds agree, stop — that is the Entry 146 failure signature.
